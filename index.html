Got Questions? We Got Answers.

Everything you need to know about setting up Menuvv.

What is a WhatsApp rate card?▼
A WhatsApp rate card is a shareable service menu with your offerings and prices. Clients open your link, select the services they want, see an estimated total, and send the request to you directly in WhatsApp.
Does it actually work?▼
Menuvv gives clients a clearer way to review your services and request a quote. It keeps the details in one place so you spend less time repeating prices in chat.
What makes Menuvv better than doing my own pricing PDFs?▼
You get interactive rate cards in seconds instead of dealing with outdated static documents or endless back-and-forth chat pricing. Menuvv calculates everything instantly so clients can order right away.
Which platforms does Menuvv work with?▼
Your rate card works anywhere you can share a link. WhatsApp is the main order channel, and you can also place the link in Instagram, TikTok, email, or your website.
How fast is the rate card setup?▼
Under a minute. You add your services, input prices, and get your clean link right away. No waiting around or manual setup needed.
Do I need technical experience to use Menuvv?▼
Not at all. Menuvv explains everything in plain English and gives you a ready-to-share link. Beginners use it to skip the learning curve and start selling professionally from day one.
Is my customer data secure?▼
Your rate card only contains the information you choose to add. Orders are sent to your WhatsApp, and Menuvv does not handle your customer payments.
Can I try before paying?▼
For sure. Get started for just $1 with our limited-time launch offer and get instant access to everything, unlimited cards, and templates. Try it out, see what you close, and then decide. Literally no risk.
How can I cancel my subscription?▼
Super simple, you can cancel right from your dashboard whenever you want. You stay in full control and it only takes a minute.
Can I use my own local currency?▼
Yes! Menuvv supports 40+ international currencies, including the currency selected for your market.
Can I update my prices later?▼
Yes, you can edit your services and regenerate or update your rate card links anytime in seconds.
